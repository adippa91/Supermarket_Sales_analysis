# Supermarket Grocery Sales Analysis

Exploratory data analysis and visualization of a retail grocery dataset 
with 9,994 orders across multiple cities, regions, and product categories.

## Charts

### 1. Monthly Sales Trend
![Monthly Sales](charts/monthly_sales.png)
Overall sales trend from 2015 to 2019 showing consistent year-over-year growth.

### 2. Top 10 Cities by Sales
![Top 10 Cities](charts/top_10_cities.png)
Kanyakumari leads with ₹706K in total sales across the dataset period.

### 3. Sales by Category & Sub Category
![Sales by Category](charts/sales_by_category.png)
Beverages and Eggs, Meat & Fish are the top performing categories. 
Health Drinks dominate sub-category sales at ₹1.05M.

### 4. Profit vs Sales by Region
![Profit vs Sales](charts/profit_vs_sales.png)
West region leads in both sales (₹4.8M) and profit (₹1.19M). 
South has the lowest performance across both metrics.

### 5. 2016 vs 2017 Monthly Comparison
![Sales Comparison](charts/sales_comparison_2016_2017.png)
2017 consistently outperformed 2016, with the gap widening 
significantly from September onwards.

## Tools Used
- Python 3
- Pandas — data cleaning and aggregation
- Matplotlib — data visualization

[Supermart Grocery Sales Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/) via Kaggle# Supermarket_Sales_analysis
